Urban electric scooter: colour, material and finish exploration

One scooter form, four colourways. Geometry, camera angle and lighting stay fixed, so only colour, material and finish change from one variant to the next.

Independent concept study. Not an official product and not affiliated with any manufacturer.

Four colourways

Each variant pairs a body colour with a trim colour and a hardware finish. Swatches show the three material treatments used in that variant.

Ivory and charcoal

Soft ivory panels against charcoal trim. Champagne hardware keeps the contrast gentle.

Sage and dark grey

A muted, slightly grey green on matte panels, grounded by dark grey trim and pale sage detailing.

Terracotta and warm cream

Cream panels carry terracotta only where the eye should land: the body stripe, column inlay and seat piping.

Graphite and silver

Satin metallic graphite panels catch broad highlights. Silver details and near-black trim keep it crisp.

Material system

Three finishes are reused across all colourways, so the range reads as one family.

Matte painted panels

Low-gloss, even surface with fine grain. Used on body panels, fender and leg shield.

Satin metallic

Soft, directional highlight with no mirror gloss. Used on hardware, wheel centres and the graphite body.

Textured dark trim

Fine cross-hatch grain that absorbs light. Used on the deck, skirt and handlebar.

Detail studies

Finish transitions
