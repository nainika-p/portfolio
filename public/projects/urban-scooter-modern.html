Urban Scooter — Design Exploration

A main concept and three variations of one urban electric scooter, exploring body shape, front lighting and proportion in a restrained palette. Independent concept study, not affiliated with any manufacturer.

Main concept

Balanced proportions, matte ivory panels and a calm, uncluttered silhouette.

  • Matte ivory body panels
  • Charcoal deck, seat and handlebar
  • Terracotta accent band
  • Slim horizontal lamp

Variations

Same camera and scale for each, so proportions compare directly.

Variation 1: Compact

Shorter wheelbase, smaller wheels and a round lamp. Charcoal body with an ivory seat.

Variation 2: Long deck

Longer deck, lower seat and a crisper, more angular body. Terracotta shield with twin vertical lamps.

Variation 3: Tall tail

Raised tail and seat on a short deck. Charcoal shield, terracotta seat and a slim full-width lamp.

Rendered from a parametric 3D model projected to vector, with matte shading and soft studio light. Colours are digital approximations. Concept exploration only, with no brand marks.
