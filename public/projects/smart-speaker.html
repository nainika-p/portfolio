Smart Speaker — Product Design Exploration

Four conceptual directions for a speaker in a contemporary home. An early-stage design exploration, not a completed project or an existing product.

Soft cylinder

Rounded, tactile and calm. Woven fabric wraps a soft-touch cap, with a thin glow at the base. Height about 1.2 × width.

Compact facet

A faceted block in matte charcoal with a precise dial on top and a micro-perforated grille. Height about 1.4 × edge width.

Sculpted oval

A pebble-like body in warm ivory with a terracotta fabric band and a soft-touch control row. Height about 1.45 × width.

Brushed bar

A rectangular body with generous curved edges, brushed metal and a recessed fabric panel. Width about 1.9 × height.

Vector-rendered studio visualisations of four conceptual forms. Colours and materials are digital approximations. No brand marks are used.
